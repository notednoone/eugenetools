Who we are

Like many of you, our members hit a point where we wanted to get out, be around others, and make some real friends. This club turned out to be a pretty good answer to that.

We are a mixed bag, and that is kind of the point. Different backgrounds, different ages, different ways of spending a weekend. Mostly guys, though not exclusively, and nobody here fits a single mold. What we share is simpler than any of that: we each wanted real connection and we were willing to show up for it.

What to expect

Show up

Take a seat, feel free to introduce yourself, or just hang out!

No pressure

Join us anytime you feel like it, skip whenever you don't.

No agenda

No icebreakers, no check-in circle. Just conversations.

Varying events
